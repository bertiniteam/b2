◆ max_num_refinements

unsigned bertini::endgame::EndgameConfig::max_num_refinements = 4

Maximum Newton refinements when sharpening endgame sample points. A tracked sample is already on the path to tracking tolerance (~1e-6), and Newton doubles the correct digits per iteration, so 4 covers any realistic refinement target (6 -> 12 -> 24 -> 48 -> 96 digits); more is never legitimately needed. (The old default of 15 was a remnant of poorly scaled random coefficient draws, since fixed by seed-rooted derivation.)
