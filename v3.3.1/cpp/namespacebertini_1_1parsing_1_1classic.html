◆ StripUTF8BOM()

void bertini::parsing::classic::StripUTF8BOM ( std::string &  s)
inline

Strip a leading UTF-8 byte-order mark (EF BB BF) from s in place, if present.

Editors (notably on Windows) may prepend a BOM; the Qi grammar treats input as raw UTF-8, so the BOM must be removed at the read boundary or it would appear as a stray leading "character".
