Detailed Description

Summary of what the midpath (path-crossing) check found at the endgame boundary, and what the algorithm did about it.

Two distinct paths landing on the same point at the endgame boundary is a probability-0 event: it signals a path crossing (under-resolved tracking), not a benign coincidence. The zero-dim algorithm detects these at the boundary using a relaxed same-point tolerance and re-tracks the offending paths with tightened settings (see ZeroDim::EGBoundaryAction). This struct records the outcome so a caller can tell whether a solve hit crossings and whether they were resolved.
