Detailed Description

Metadata produced by a single predict or correct step.

Collapses the formerly hand-threaded out-parameters (norm_J, norm_J_inverse, condition_number_estimate, size_proportion, error_estimate, norm_delta_z) into one struct. Not every field is written by every step: size_proportion/error_estimate are predictor-only (and error_estimate only for embedded methods); norm_delta_z is corrector-only. Unwritten fields keep their default of 0.
